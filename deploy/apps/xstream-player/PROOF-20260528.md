## Scope

metadata/buffer bytes but remain effectively stuck, muted, or delayed after the
user clicked `Play Movie`.

## Change

- `components/VideoPlayer.tsx` now starts audible playback immediately after the
  source is ready instead of waiting for a large startup buffer first.
- The large HLS/progressive buffers remain in place for smooth playback after
  playback starts.
- Autoplay now explicitly forces `muted=false` and a non-zero volume, then falls
  back to visible controls if the browser blocks the play call.
- `scripts/xstream-random-2026-movie-proof.mjs` was tightened so it only passes
  when the video is unpaused, unmuted, has volume, and `currentTime` advances.
